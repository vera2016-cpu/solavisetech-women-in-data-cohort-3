# Solavisetech Women in Data — Cohort 3

## About the Program

The **Solavisetech Women in Data Cohort 3** program is a learning and development opportunity designed to help participants build their knowledge and practical skills in data. It provides an opportunity to learn relevant techniques, gain hands-on experience, and understand how data can be used to solve real-world problems.

This repository will serve as my personal learning portfolio throughout the program. I will use it to document my progress, learning notes, assignments, practical exercises, and projects.

## My Goals for the Program

By the end of this program, I aim to:

- **Strengthen my data analysis skills:** Learn how to collect, clean, analyze, and interpret data.
- **Improve my technical skills:** Develop proficiency in relevant data tools, programming languages, and analytical techniques.
- **Work on practical projects:** Apply the knowledge gained to real-world datasets and meaningful problems.
- **Improve data visualization and storytelling:** Present insights clearly using charts, reports, and evidence-based recommendations.
- **Develop problem-solving skills:** Learn to use data to answer questions and support informed decisions.
- **Build a professional portfolio:** Showcase my assignments and projects to demonstrate my abilities to potential employers.
- **Connect with other professionals:** Learn from mentors, collaborate with fellow participants, and grow my professional network.

## My Learning Journey

I plan to organize my work in this repository as I progress through the program.

- `assignments/` — Program exercises and assignments.
- `projects/` — Practical data analysis and machine learning projects.
- `notes/` — Learning notes, important concepts, and key takeaways.
- `visualizations/` — Charts and other visual outputs from my analyses.

These folders will be added as my work develops.

## What I Hope to Achieve

At the end of the program, I hope to have a stronger understanding of data workflows, practical experience working with datasets, completed projects that demonstrate my skills, and a portfolio that supports my career goals in data analytics and artificial intelligence.

My goal is not just to complete the program, but to become a more confident, capable, and career-ready data professional who can use data to create meaningful impact.

## My Commitment

I am committed to learning consistently, practicing what I learn, asking questions, collaborating with others, and documenting my progress throughout the program.

I look forward to making the most of this opportunity and growing through the Solavisetech Women in Data Cohort 3 journey.

**One step at a time. One project at a time. Continuous growth.**
